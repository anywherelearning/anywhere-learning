# Pinterest Keyword Bank — Anywhere Learning

**Rewritten 11 August 2026.** The April version was built from general niche knowledge and never tested. This version leads with the phrases that actually earned clicks on this account between 13 May and 11 Aug 2026, then keeps the untested seed terms in a clearly separate section so you know which is which.

Read [STRATEGY-PINTEREST.md](STRATEGY-PINTEREST.md) first. It explains why the title shapes below are the ones that work.

---

## How to use this doc

- **Pin title:** take a proven shape from Section 1, swap in the topic. Lead with the keyword in the first three words.
- **Pin description:** repeat the same long-tail phrase in the first 50-60 characters, in a natural sentence. Then two or three related sub-topics woven in as prose.
- **Board description:** 5-8 phrases from the relevant section, primary keyword in the first 50-60 characters, 500 characters total, no hashtags.
- **Do not stuff.** Pinterest reads meaning. A keyword list reads as spam and ranks worse than a clean sentence.

## 1. Proven shapes (earned clicks on this account)

These are ranked by outbound clicks over the 90-day window. Reuse the shape, change the topic.

### Shape A: `[Number] [Topic] for Kids by Age (Free Printable Checklist)`

The single best-performing construction on the account. 47 clicks at a 5.7% click rate, 12.6% engagement rate.

- 28 life skills for kids by age
- 24 age-appropriate chores by age
- 16 engineering challenges for kids
- 50 nature walk ideas for kids
- 15 backyard science experiments for kids
- 30 kitchen activities for kids
- 26 creative and maker ideas for kids
- 18 AI and digital literacy activities for kids
- 12 resilience-building activities for kids
- 17 seasonal scavenger hunt ideas
- 13 forest school activities
- 22 travel and worldschool ideas for kids
- 11 real-world history ideas for kids

**Why it works:** the number promises scope, "by age" matches how parents search, and the parenthetical names a concrete free artifact. All three survive truncation.

### Shape B: `How to Homeschool [Grade] Without a Curriculum`

Second best. 14 clicks at 2.6%. Answers an anxiety rather than describing a topic.

- how to homeschool kindergarten without a curriculum
- how to homeschool 1st grade without a curriculum
- how to homeschool middle school without a curriculum
- homeschool without a curriculum
- homeschool first year checklist
- new to homeschooling

**Gap worth noting:** only kindergarten and 1st grade exist as posts. 2nd through 5th grade are unwritten, and this is the second-best converting shape on the account.

### Shape C: `[Practice] at Home (What It Really Looks Like)`

Lower volume, high engagement rate (8.7%). Works on process and reassurance topics.

- project-based learning at home
- deschooling activities
- hands-on learning at home
- unschooling vs homeschooling
- what project-based learning really looks like

### Shape D: `Hands-On [Skill] Activities for Kids`

Small volume but the best engagement rate on the account at 17.4%, and 6.5% click rate.

- hands-on resilience activities for kids
- hands-on outdoor STEM activities for kids
- hands-on engineering projects for kids
- hands-on money activities for kids
- hands-on life skills for kids

- secular homeschool
- worldschooling / world schooling
- homeschool day in the life

## 5. Terms to avoid

Brand copy rules, and they apply here too. These match searches but fight the positioning:

- curriculum-aligned, worksheets, busywork, teacher resources
- academic performance, accelerate learning
- anything framing the activities as kid-solo (they are parent-led)

`homeschool printables` is the one deliberate exception. It is a search term, used in pin metadata only, never in the pin copy itself.
