After fifteen years as a teacher, here's the thing I'm most sure of: kids don't need more worksheets to learn math. They need a reason to do it.

Take Party Planner Math, one of the guides in the library. Your kid plans a real party on a real budget. How many cups for twelve guests? Does the recipe need doubling? Is the big bag of chips actually cheaper? That's multiplication, division, scaling and comparing prices, and they work hard at it because it's their party and the money is real.

Every activity in the library works like that. Real math, real writing, real planning, tucked inside something your family actually does together. And the life skills (budgeting, planning ahead, making trade-offs) come along for free.
Open and follow along. You open a guide on any device and follow the steps with your kid. No printing, no prep night, and each one has levels so siblings can do it together.
