On the screen thing, here's where I've landed. The goal was never zero. It's a kid who can put it down, question what it shows them, and use it to make something instead of just scroll. That's a skill, and skills get practiced, not banned. So this month we're taking one hour a day back as a family and keeping a running list on the fridge of what we did instead. Not less screen time for its own sake. Just noticing what one reclaimed hour adds up to.

One more thing. I'm running something free in September, five days, no cost, and it's the first time I've done it. Watch your inbox the first week of the month.
