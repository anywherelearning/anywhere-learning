Pack Like a Pro
Time
about 45 min

A bag
Who leads
Them, alone
{{ message_content }}

Good morning. Today you hand over the wheel.

Your kid packs a bag for a trip. Real or invented, it doesn’t matter. You give them the scenario and one hard limit: one bag, and it has to close.

Pick a scenario:

  • A day hike, home before dark. The gentlest one. Good for younger kids.
  • Two nights at a friend’s or a grandparent’s. The classic. Sleeping, teeth, two days of clothes.
  • A beach day, and the forecast can’t make up its mind. Forces them to pack for two different days at once.
  • A week somewhere cold, one bag. The hardest. Bulk against count, and there’s no clean answer.

Then they have to defend it. Why that and not this. What happens if it rains. What did you leave out, and why.

A twist, if you want one
Once the bag is packed and shut, change the situation on them. “Actually, we’re staying an extra night.” Then watch. Repacking with new information is a harder and more useful skill than packing was, and it’s the one that shows up everywhere else in life.

What’s actually happening: this one looks like nothing and it’s the deepest activity of the week. Packing is thinking ahead, making trade-offs, and imagining a version of yourself in a situation that hasn’t happened yet. That’s executive function, the exact thing everybody worries their kid is missing.

They will forget socks. Let them forget socks.

The forgetting is the memory that teaches. Don’t fix the bag.

When you’re done: post what they left out. It’s always something excellent.

Tomorrow is the big one, so here’s your heads up. Day 5 needs:

  • Some cash, in your kid’s hand. Twenty is the default
  • A trip to a grocery store together
  • An hour or so, plus however long the eating takes

They’re shopping for snacks and something sweet to share tonight, not making dinner from scratch, so twenty usually goes far enough. Pick whatever number is real for your week. Ten is fine. Thirty is fine if groceries are brutal where you are. It just has to be fixed before you go, and a little tight.

Get the cash tonight so tomorrow is easy.
