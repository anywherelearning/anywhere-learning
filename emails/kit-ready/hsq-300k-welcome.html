The short version of me: I was a teacher for fifteen years. Then I took a year off, traveled with my two kids and homeschooled them on the road. Now we homeschool at home part of the year and worldschool the rest.

That year showed me how much learning hides inside real things that need doing. So that’s what I build. Anywhere Learning is a library of real-world activities for kids about 6 to 14, where the math, the writing, the science and the life skills happen in the same task. Running a tiny business. Planning a trip on a real budget. You open a guide on any device and do it together, with very little prep.
