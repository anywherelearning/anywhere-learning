Last month I said I was running something free in September. Here it is.

If you’ve been wanting more real-world learning in your days, the kind where your kid is building, deciding, writing and figuring things out because the task is real, and life skills grow right alongside the academics, this is your week to try it. And if you want learning to actually be fun again, for both of you, even better.

Five days, one real-world activity a day, about an hour each. Monday September 14 to Friday September 18. Nothing to buy, nothing to print, nothing to prep the night before.

Every morning you get an email from me with that day’s activity. You do it with your kid, not near them, and that part matters more than anything else I could tell you. Then you pop into our Facebook group, post a photo or one line about how it went, and I reply. I’m doing all five with my own two kids on the same days. Flops included.

What your kid will be working on
Writing and communication. Putting an idea into words clearly enough that someone else can follow it, then making a case and listening to the other side.
STEM and problem-solving. Building something real outside, testing it, watching it fail, fixing it.
Critical thinking. Forming an opinion, backing it up, and changing it when the other side has a point.
Planning and independence. Running a real job start to finish while you sit on your hands.
Real-world math and money. A real budget, real trade-offs, and a decision that actually counts.

Five days, five areas of the Future-Ready Skills Map, and the learning is built right into the doing. Real things, done together, that your kid will actually remember.

One heads-up: Friday needs twenty dollars in cash and a trip to a store with your kid. You’ve got time to plan for it.
