# Worksheet tool fonts

Self-hosted so the live SVG preview and the jsPDF download render from the exact
same files. Each is redistributable for commercial web use.

## Andika-Regular.ttf ("Print")

Andika by SIL International. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Designed specifically for literacy learners, with unambiguous letterforms
(single-storey a, distinct l / I / 1).
https://software.sil.org/andika/

## EduAUVICWANTPre.ttf ("Pre-cursive")

Edu AU VIC WA NT Pre by Australian Type Foundry, via Google Fonts.
Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The Victorian / WA / NT school style: entry and exit strokes on each letter,
which become the joins when children move on to full cursive.
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Edu+AU+VIC+WA+NT+Pre

## LearningCurve.ttf ("Cursive")

Learning Curve 4.0 by Jess Latham, Blue Vinyl Fonts. Freeware: "free to use in
any private and commercial manner", may not be resold or remarketed, and may not
be included on CD-ROM compilations. Genuinely joined school cursive.
http://bvfonts.com/

Note on the Google Fonts "Edu" family: several members whose names contain
"Cursive" or "Beginner" are in fact unjoined print styles. Edu NSW ACT Cursive
and Edu QLD Beginner were both trialled here and rejected for that reason.
