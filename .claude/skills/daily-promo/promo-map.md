## Facebook groups

### Worldschoolers
- Private, 74.8K members. Big and active. Perfect audience fit (worldschool families).
- Promo rules: promo only in admin threads.
- Promo days: **Monday** ("Money Monday" thread: business ads, services) and **Friday** ("Follow Friday" thread: post your Instagram, YouTube, blogs, whatever).
- What to promote: Money Monday = membership or free guide as the business pitch. Follow Friday = @anywherelearning IG or a blog post (softer, do this weekly). Alternate what Follow Friday links to.
- Notes: also a great place for genuine travel/worldschool value replies between promo days.

### Haven & Ember Homeschool International
- Private, 242.9K members. The biggest reach of the list.
- Promo rules: promo in the weekly admin thread only. Friday has a "Community Connections" thread (relational, not sales).
- Promo day: **Saturday** ("Small Business Promotions" thread). Answer the admin's framing questions when present (what makes your business different, why should this community choose you).
- What to promote: free guide or membership. Lead with the founder story angle (15 yrs teaching, chose homeschooling), it fits this group's "parents are the most equipped" ethos.

### Homeschool & Creative Learning for Parents and Teachers
- Public, 13.5K members, very active (500+ posts/month).
- Promo rules: no spam. Resource Sharing Rule: educational resources may be shared directly in the group anytime IF shared in-group with enough info to be useful on its own (no "comment to get the link" bait). Links to blogs/stores allowed when relevant and providing value. Beta testing only in the Feedback Friday thread (must disclose free/beta/paid).
- Promo days: **Sunday** ("Small Business Sunday" thread by admin Elise Galperin). Thread rules: ONE share per person, own work only, must directly support education/literacy/homeschooling/family learning, no affiliate links, and pick at least one other small business to support (visit page, ask a question, recommend, share). Never drop-a-link-and-disappear.
- What to promote: Sunday = shop/membership/seasonal offer. Anytime = a genuinely useful blog post shared as a resource with real context.
- Notes: the "support another business" step is mandatory. The reminder must include it.

### Homeschool for You Resources, Curriculum, and More!
- Private, 2.1K members. Small but promo-friendly.
- Promo rules: all promotional posts go in the promo thread only.
- Promo days: promo thread posted **every Saturday and Monday** ("Post all education-related promos here: websites, products, shops, socials").
- What to promote: rotate freely (free guide, shop, blog, IG). Low stakes, good place to test copy.

### Éducation à domicile 101: école-maison, unschooling, alternatives
- Private, 5.4K members, Quebec/Canada. Low activity (~29 posts/month). **Post in French.**
- Promo rules: strict. A first post that is pure advertising = removal from the group. Offers of products or services (free or paid) go as a COMMENT under the weekly "Découvertes du lundi" post. Full details in the pinned post: https://m.facebook.com/groups/EducationADomicile101/permalink/3120317118195925/
- Promo day: **Monday** (comment under "Découvertes du lundi").
- What to promote: free guide or blog, in French, low-key. Build standing with real participation before pushing membership.

### Homeschooling 101: Help for Homeschool Parents
- Public, 6.9K members, US-based. https://www.facebook.com/groups/homeschooling101.net · admin Jennifer Rooks MacDonald.
- Promo rules (verified in-group 2026-09-01): NO business posts and NO promotion posts, ever (rule change Jul 16 2026). **No links in posts at all** (rule change Jun 2 2026). Links in COMMENTS are fine, that is where all promo lives.
- Two comment slots, both on admin posts:
  1. **Monthly Promotion Post** ("What do you have for sale or service?"), posted around the 1st. Typically 25-57 comments. → **1st of the month.**
  2. **"WELCOME New Members!!!" post**, which says "If you are a business or promoting your 'brand', comment on this post." This is the business-listing slot, NOT a permanent pinned resources post: it RECURS every 1-2 weeks (seen May 28, Aug 11, Aug 15, Aug 31) and each copy carries only ~1 comment, so there is no house format to match. → treat as a **mid-month** slot, comment on the newest copy only.
- Also allowed: relevant blog links in comments when they genuinely fit someone's question. Free items may be posted once a month.
- Finding either post: group search (magnifying glass inside the group) for "Monthly Promotion" or "WELCOME New Members", take the newest result. Featured/announcements only holds the old welcome post, don't look there.
- What to promote: 1st = membership (founder story + collab offer). Mid-month welcome post = free guide.

## Standing rules for all groups
